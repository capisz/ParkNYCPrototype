NYC Parking Planner

Draft accessibility statement

Prototype notice: This statement requires City accessibility approval and a named contact before release.

NYC Parking Planner is being built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Every parking color has a text label, map information is also available as a list, controls work with a keyboard, and reduced-motion settings are respected.

Transit accessibility

Step-free park-and-ride results will remain unavailable until station accessibility and current elevator-outage feeds pass validation. A route will never be described as step-free when those sources are stale.

Report a problem

A production accessibility contact and response process must be published before the pilot opens. Test reports should include the page, device, browser, and assistive technology.
