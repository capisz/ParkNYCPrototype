NYC Parking Planner

Draft terms and advisory notice

Prototype notice: These terms require City legal approval before public release.

NYC Parking Planner provides advisory guidance. It is not a legal determination, parking authorization, reservation, payment service, or enforcement decision.

  • Always check all posted signs and curb markings.
  • Follow meter and ParkNYC instructions.
  • Confirm facility prices, terms, capacity, and space availability directly.
  • Confirm current transit service before travel.

A colored curb describes recognized rules for a planned time interval. It does not mean a physical space is open. Gray means the service lacks enough approved evidence and is never a recommendation.

Features may be disabled when a source is stale, unavailable, or has not passed agency validation.
